Why We Built
BacktestLab Pro

Raw historical CSV data is everywhere for free, but turning flat spreadsheets into actionable visual strategy testing is difficult for most traders. We bridged that gap with an intuitive, 100% free web workspace.

The CSV & Paywall Problem

Most historical market data (Crypto, Forex, Equities) is actually freely available in CSV files online. However, many traders find downloading, cleaning, and coding custom backtesting scripts for standard spreadsheets frustrating or overly technical.

Meanwhile, commercial retail software platforms charge heavy recurring monthly fees just to give you basic replay buttons and simple statistical dashboards. We wanted to end predatory subscriptions for basic utility tools.

Our Free Web Platform

BacktestLab Pro ingests vast historical datasets and serves them inside a smooth, interactive web browser interface. You don't need to know Python, manage messy spreadsheets, or pay monthly software bills.

Our web replay tool, multi-timeframe analytics, and metric calculation engines are completely free for community use without ad pollution or hidden limits.

What is Algorithmic Trading and How Does a Bot Really Work?

At its core, a trading bot is not artificial magic or an infallible crystal ball. An algorithm is simply a deterministic mathematical state machine designed to systematically execute trades based on rule-based conditions without human intervention.

1. Perception Engine

Reads incoming tick data, bid/ask spreads, and candle timestamps. Computes mathematical indicators (moving averages, Relative Volume, ATR) on closed bars.

2. Logic Evaluation

Checks if conditions are met simultaneously (e.g., price sweeps Asian Low + RVOL > 1.5 + current time is within London session + spread < 25 points).

3. Execution & Risk

Calculates dynamic lot size based on fixed account percentage risk, submits the market or limit order with hardcoded SL/TP, and monitors the position.

Are Public Bots Always Good? The Danger of "Black Boxes" & Scams

No. In fact, 95% of commercial trading bots sold on the internet are financial landmines. Understanding why prevents you from losing your savings to deceptive marketing:

The Martingale & Grid Illusion

Shady vendors sell bots boasting "99% win rates" by doubling position sizes every time price moves against them without ever setting a stop loss. The equity curve looks like a straight line up until one unexpected black-swan trend wipes the entire balance to zero overnight.

Overfitting & Curve-Fitting

When an author tunes 50 arbitrary parameters specifically to fit past price noise perfectly, the bot produces a beautiful backtest on historical data but fails completely the moment live market conditions shift.

Execution Realism: Solving the Intrabar Repainting Dilemma

The primary risk in backtesting is divergence between simulated and live performance[cite: 11]. If an engine evaluates entry triggers on active candles using close[0], the trade may show an inflated historical win rate that cannot be executed in live markets[cite: 11].

BacktestLab Pro addresses this by enforcing closed-bar validation (barstate.isconfirmed == true) across all simulated multi-timeframe streams[cite: 11]. Orders execute at the open of the following candle, incorporating bid-ask spread and slippage buffers[cite: 11].

Frequently Asked Questions

Why backtest before paying for a bot?

Most automation projects fail because the underlying idea was never mechanically tested across enough data. BacktestLab Pro allows you to refine your rules completely free so you only pay for executable files once your edge is proven.

What do I receive when I export the AI bot?

You receive the clean, compiled and raw script files configured for your chosen broker platform (such as MetaTrader .ex5/.mq5 or Pine Script source code), ready to attach to your charts.

When should I choose the 7-Day Developer Service?

If your strategy involves complex non-standard conditions (like multi-timeframe order blocks, proprietary data feeds, or custom risk-hedging algorithms), our human quant developers refine your logic and deliver a bulletproof engine within 7 working days.

01

Prompt Dissection & Parameter Extraction (Semantic Tokenization)

When you enter a conversational trading hypothesis into the BYO-AI console, standard large language models often struggle to produce deterministic trading code due to hallucination or syntactic inconsistencies[cite: 2]. BacktestLab Pro constrains the output using structured JSON schema schemas. The engine breaks down your prompt into four isolated parameter nodes:

1. Market Condition Trigger

Detects structural states: Asian Range high/low breaks, London Open liquidity sweeps, PDH/PDL tests, or Fair Value Gap (FVG) mitigations.

2. Quantitative Confirmation

Evaluates mathematical filters: Relative Volume expansion (RVOL > 1.5), EMA trend alignment, or ATR volatility thresholds.

3. Exact Invalidation & Risk

Enforces stop loss logic: structural swing extremes, fixed point limits, or percentage-of-equity risk budgeting per order.

4. Profit Realization Rules

Defines exit criteria: mathematical Risk-to-Reward targets (e.g., 2.0R to 3.5R), trailing ATR stops, or opposite session band touches.

02

Client-Side Historical Execution & Lookahead Elimination

Unlike web platforms that calculate backtests on high-level daily bars or simplified close prices, BacktestLab Pro passes generated rules directly into an in-browser event-driven backtesting engine[cite: 2].

Candle State Sequence Emulation Zero Lookahead Bias

Each historical bar is processed through an intrabar sequence: Open → High/Low based on the candle's directional expansion → Close. If a candle's wick touches your Stop Loss before your Take Profit during a bearish candle, the trade is marked as a loss immediately—reproducing live exchange outcomes.

Slippage & Dynamic Spread Friction Execution Realism

Market orders and breakout stop entries suffer from fill latency in live conditions. The engine allows you to configure point slippage and variable spreads to verify that your edge survives transaction costs.

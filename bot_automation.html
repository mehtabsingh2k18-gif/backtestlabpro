FAQ

Bot Automation & BYO-AI Questions

Do I have to pay BacktestLab for AI usage?

No. You provide your own API key. You only pay your AI provider directly for the tokens you consume. BacktestLab Pro charges nothing to prompt, compile, or backtest.

Are my strategy rules kept private?

Yes. All strategy compilation with your BYO-AI key runs client-side in your browser workspace. Your logic is not logged or shared with third parties.

When do I pay for production code?

Only when you have thoroughly validated your strategy across historical data and want the final .mql5 or .py files for your live MetaTrader terminal or VPS.

Frequently Asked Questions

Strategy Testing, Bot Concurrency & Code Downloads

Is BacktestLab Pro really free, or is it a limited free tier?

The core platform is 100% free with no session timeouts, daily execution limits, or chart throttles. You can replay multi-year historical feeds and test community bots side-by-side without a credit card.

How does the Bring Your Own API (BYO-API) bot feature work?

You can plug in your own API keys to generate custom bot logic. The compiled execution model runs directly inside your browser session and saves to your workspace, ensuring your proprietary strategy rules never get exposed to third parties.

Can I test multiple bots on the chart at the same time?

Yes. Our v2.0 engine allows you to drag multiple bots directly onto the candle replay canvas. Each bot runs in an isolated background thread with its own virtual balance and trade markers, letting you compare performance head-to-head.

When do I need to pay?

Replaying charts and simulating bot behavior is completely free. Paid tiers apply only when you choose to download the raw, production-ready code files (like .mql5 Expert Advisors or Python scripts) to execute on live brokerage accounts.

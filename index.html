01

Intrabar Tick Feeds, Data Chunking & Replay Mechanics

Most browser-based charting tools suffer from memory leaks or freeze when loading high-resolution data. BacktestLab Pro solves this by implementing dynamic client-side historical chunking. The engine streams raw institutional tick data spanning up to 20+ years for Gold (XAUUSD), major currency pairs (EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY), benchmark equity indices, and spot cryptocurrencies.

Candle Step-Forward Logic

Traders can advance bars manually one-by-one or engage the throttled auto-stepper at variable intervals (0.5x to 10x real-time). Intrabar movement checks stop loss (SL) and take profit (TP) triggers using strict bar sequence emulation (Open → High/Low priority based on candle direction → Close) to prevent historical lookahead bias.

State Preservation & Memory

Every candle state, active trade, and historical equity marker is cached in local browser memory. Refreshing or accidentally closing the browser does not wipe your session progress; your backtest resumes instantly at the exact bar index you left off.

02

Liquidity Sweeps, Asian Range & PDH/PDL Validation

Discretionary price action models depend heavily on market session transitions and key structural reference points. BacktestLab Pro includes native calculation logic for session high/low breakouts and institutional range mitigations.

Asian Session Range (00:00 – 06:00 UTC) Session Liquidity

The engine automatically tracks the absolute high and low established during Tokyo trading hours. When London or New York opens, candle breaches beyond these levels trigger liquidity sweep flags. Traders can backtest the classic liquidity sweep: price piercing the Asian extreme, failing to sustain acceptance, and printing an aggressive rejection candle back inside the range.

Previous Day High & Low (PDH / PDL) Macro Structural Levels

Prior day extremes serve as major resting pools for institutional stop orders. BacktestLab Pro computes the previous 24-hour high and low automatically on daily closes, projecting them as key support/resistance zones onto 1M, 5M, and 15M intraday charts for break-and-retest or fakeout validation.

Relative Volume (RVOL) Breakout Verification Volume Expansion

To eliminate false breakouts during low-liquidity chop, the platform calculates Relative Volume: RVOL = Current Bar Volume / 20-Period Simple Moving Average of Volume Breakout signals can be programmatically gated by requiring an RVOL > 1.5, ensuring candle expansions carry verifiable institutional volume before triggering an entry.

Proprietary trading strategies are valuable intellectual property. Most AI-assisted platforms store user prompts, parameters, and strategy logic on central cloud servers. BacktestLab Pro utilizes a local-first, client-side sandbox architecture.

Deterministic Simulation & Execution Framework

A technical breakdown of data handling, market structure calculations, and execution parameters governing BacktestLab Pro.

02

Structural Price Action & Session Liquidity Logic

Discretionary market structure trading relies on explicit session bounds and previous daily ranges. BacktestLab Pro projects structural liquidity markers directly onto intraday replay charts.

Asian Session Range (00:00 – 06:00 UTC) Session Boundary

The platform tracks the established high and low during the Asian session. When European or US markets open, price extensions outside this range trigger visual sweep flags, allowing traders to test mean reversion and Asian range sweep setups.

Previous Day High & Low (PDH / PDL) Structural Reference

Prior-day extremes serve as macro benchmark zones for resting order flow. The engine computes daily closes dynamically, projecting PDH and PDL levels as horizontal lines on 1M, 5M, and 15M charts for break-and-retest strategies.

Relative Volume (RVOL) Verification Volume Context

To filter out low-liquidity fakeouts, Relative Volume calculates the ratio of the current bar volume against its 20-period moving average: RVOL = Current Bar Volume / SMA(Volume, 20) Traders can establish volume confirmation rules (e.g., RVOL > 1.5) before validating breakout entries.

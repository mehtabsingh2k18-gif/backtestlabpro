Frequently Asked Questions

Free Backtesting Tools, Historical Data & Replay Limits

Is BacktestLab Pro really free, or just a limited free tier?

BacktestLab Pro is 100% free in reality—not an expiring free tier or trial trap. You get unhindered access to bar replay tools, unrestricted trade counts, lower-timeframe simulations, and deep multi-year archives without any subscription fee or credit card on file.

How many years of historical data are available for backtesting?

Data depth depends on the instrument: major Forex spot pairs and Gold (XAUUSD) provide up to 20+ years of data, while equities offer 12 to 15+ years. Select major cryptocurrency pairs feature complete lifetime historical data tracing back to initial exchange listings.

Are there any session timeouts, daily trade limits, or bar caps?

No. You can run as many simulation sessions as you wish, execute hundreds of simulated orders, and test multi-month or multi-year swing and day strategies without artificial limits or session kickouts.

Do I need to sign up to test my trading strategies?

No sign-up is required to jump into the charting engine. You can launch the simulator instantly as a guest. Account creation is completely optional and only needed if you wish to store, synchronize, and track long-term performance matrices across devices.

How to backtest trading strategies for free on XAUUSD and Crypto?

Click Open Free Simulator or select an asset from our Historical Data Pool. Choose your timeframe (1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, or Daily), choose any historical anchor point from our multi-year dataset, and advance candles bar-by-bar using the manual replay controls to validate your edge in real-time.

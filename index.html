Philosophy & Rigor

Any strategy can be researched. You decide whether it's good enough.

We don't decide whether your strategy deserves automation. Win rate alone never determines if an algorithm is viable. Expectancy, drawdown distributions, and risk-to-reward ratios do. Our platform provides unbiased historical simulation so you can validate performance on raw market data.

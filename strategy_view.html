Trader Psychology, Tilt Prevention & Drawdowns

Mental protocols to eliminate hesitation, revenge trading, and fear
1. Navigating Loss Streaks

Every quantitative edge is probabilistic. A strategy with a 65% win rate will reliably experience 4 to 6 consecutive losing trades multiple times a year. Intervening manually during normal statistical variances breaks algorithmic expectancy.

2. Eradicating FOMO & Chasing

If price shoots ahead without fulfilling your confirmation criteria, let it go. Missing a move costs zero dollars; entering without confirmation deteriorates risk-reward ratios and exposes your account to liquidity sweeps.

3. Invalidation Discipline

Stop losses protect your capital from market dislocations. Widening a stop loss in the hope of an impending bounce is the primary reason accounts fail. Accept the loss cleanly as a regular operational business expense.
